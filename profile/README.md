# Xkye Solutions

**One system that fits how your business already works.**

Xkye Solutions is a solutions studio. We turn the spreadsheets, chat threads and workarounds running your operations into software built around you, and we keep it fitting as you grow.

[Visit xkye.solutions →](https://xkye.solutions)

## What we do

- **Custom business systems**: operations tools, internal systems and portals, built around how your team actually works.
- **Product design & development**: apps taken from idea to launch, designed, built and shipped with you.
- **Fractional CTO**: senior technical leadership without a full-time hire. Architecture, build-or-buy calls, and a roadmap you can trust.

## Work

- **Renigo**: a resident app for residential communities, replacing guardhouse calls, paper logbooks and group-chat notices with one calm app. [Read the case study →](https://renigo.xkye.app)
- **Kova**: Xkye's own project, issue and knowledge tool. Issues, plans and documents in one place that uses the same words everywhere, in the browser and in the terminal. [Read the case study →](https://kova.xkye.app)
